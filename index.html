Our Mission

We believe learning should never be locked behind a fee, a login wall or a slow internet connection. EduConnect was built during a college Social Internship to turn that belief into something usable: a single offline-friendly hub of videos, scholarships and honest career advice for school and undergraduate students who are figuring things out on their own.

SDG 4

Why SDG 4 — Quality Education?

Goal 4 of the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goals calls for inclusive, equitable, quality education and lifelong learning opportunities for all, by 2030. It recognises that education is the single most powerful lever a person has to improve their own life — and their community's.

  • Ensure free, equitable primary and secondary education for all girls and boys
  • Increase access to affordable technical, vocational and higher education
  • Eliminate gender and wealth disparities in access to learning
  • Build a global generation of learners equipped for decent work

Scholarships

A shortlist of scholarships worth checking — verify current details on each official website before applying.

National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship

Eligibility: Class 9–12 students from economically weaker sections with 55%+ marks.

Benefits: ₹12,000 per year until Class 12.

Last Date: Varies by state — check official portal.

Apply / Learn More

INSPIRE Scholarship (DST)

Eligibility: Top 1% in Class 12 boards, pursuing B.Sc/Integrated M.Sc.

Benefits: ₹80,000 per year for 5 years.

Last Date: Usually October–November each year.

Apply / Learn More

Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST/OBC

Eligibility: Students from SC/ST/OBC categories in post-matric courses.

Benefits: Full tuition fee reimbursement + maintenance allowance.

Last Date: Typically December, state-dependent.

Apply / Learn More

Kishore Vaigyanik Protsahan Yojana (KVPY-successor)

Eligibility: Class 11/12 & UG students inclined towards research in Science.

Benefits: Monthly fellowship + annual contingency grant.

Last Date: Announced yearly on the DST portal.

Apply / Learn More

Girl Child Education Scholarship

Eligibility: Girl students in government schools, Class 6–12.

Benefits: ₹3,000–₹5,000 per year & bicycle assistance in select states.

Last Date: Varies by state education department.

Apply / Learn More

Private CSR Merit Scholarships

Eligibility: Varies — typically merit + income based, offered by corporate foundations.

Benefits: Ranges from ₹10,000 to full course fee.

Last Date: Rolling — check individual foundation sites.

Apply / Learn More

Career Guidance

Honest, practical starting points for six common career directions after school.

⚙️ Engineering

Eligibility: Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, Maths (PCM); JEE / state CET.

Skills Needed: Problem-solving, mathematics, logical reasoning, curiosity for how things work.

Opportunities: Core engineering, software development, research, entrepreneurship, higher studies abroad.

🩺 Medical

Eligibility: Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology (PCB); NEET qualification.

Skills Needed: Patience, empathy, memory retention, resilience under pressure.

Opportunities: MBBS, BDS, nursing, allied health sciences, research, public health.

💹 Commerce

Eligibility: Class 12 in any stream (Commerce preferred) for B.Com/BBA/CA/CS.

Skills Needed: Numerical aptitude, attention to detail, ethics, communication.

Opportunities: Chartered Accountancy, banking, finance, entrepreneurship, company secretary roles.

🎨 Arts & Humanities

Eligibility: Class 12 in any stream for BA in literature, history, psychology, design, etc.

Skills Needed: Communication, creativity, critical thinking, cultural awareness.

Opportunities: Civil services, journalism, design, law, teaching, social work, content creation.

🏛️ Government Jobs

Eligibility: Varies by exam — UPSC, SSC, state PSC, banking exams, defence services.

Skills Needed: Discipline, current affairs awareness, time management, consistency in preparation.

Opportunities: Civil services, public sector banks, defence forces, railways, teaching posts.

🛠️ Skill Development

Eligibility: Open to all — school dropouts to graduates, via ITI, polytechnic or short-term courses.

Skills Needed: Hands-on aptitude, willingness to learn a trade, discipline.

Opportunities: Electrician, plumbing, tailoring, digital marketing, freelancing, self-employment.

About EduConnect

The story, mission and objectives behind this initiative.

What is EduConnect?

EduConnect is a non-commercial, student-built educational platform created as part of a college Social Internship programme. It brings together curated learning resources, video lessons, scholarship listings and career guidance in one lightweight, offline-friendly website — free for anyone to use, with nothing to sign up for.

Our Mission

To reduce the distance between a student and the resources they need — regardless of their school, location or income — by making trustworthy educational material easy to find and free to use.

Our Vision

A future where every learner, in every classroom or without one, has an equal starting point: access to quality educational material as a right, not a privilege.

Our Objectives

  • Curate reliable external learning resources instead of recreating what already works well
  • Simplify access to scholarships that students often don't hear about in time
  • Demystify career paths with plain, judgement-free guidance
  • Build a small, local volunteer network to keep the initiative growing

Contribution to SDG 4

Every part of this website maps directly to a target under UN Sustainable Development Goal 4: removing cost barriers (free resources), improving access (offline-first design, no login), and building relevant skills for further study and work (video lessons and career guidance). It is a small, local attempt at a global goal.
